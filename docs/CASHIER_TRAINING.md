# Cashier Training

Multi-user shop guide for daily POS operations.

## Open the till

- Open **Sales Desk → Cashier Shifts** and start a shift with the cash already in the drawer.
- Keep that shift open until you close at the end of the day.

## Make a sale

- Open **POS**.
- Scan a barcode, type a SKU, or search by product name.
- Confirm quantities, then charge with cash, M-Pesa, card, or bank transfer.
- For cash, enter the amount received so the change is calculated.
- Print or keep the 80mm receipt for the customer.

## Sales history

- Use **Sales Desk → Sales History** to find a sale by receipt number, customer, payment, or date.
- POS tickets start with `POS-`. Online orders stay on the same list with an Online label.
- Reprint a POS receipt from the sale page.

## Returns

- Use **Sales Desk → Returns**, look up the POS receipt, and enter the quantity coming back.
- Stock is restored immediately.
- Refund cash, M-Pesa, or the original payment method as directed.

## Close the shift

- Count the cash in the drawer.
- Close the shift and enter the actual cash. The system compares that with expected cash from opening float plus cash sales minus cash refunds.
- A negative difference means the drawer is short.

## Reports

- **Today** and **This month** open the sales report for that period.
- **Cashier Performance** shows tickets, net sales, payment mix, returns, and shift variance by cashier.
